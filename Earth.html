BASIC FUN FACTS Earth is the third planet from the Sun. It is about 4.5 billion years old. Earth is the only known planet with life. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon. A day on Earth lasts about 24 hours. A year on Earth lasts about 365 days. Earth has a protective atmosphere. Earth is the only planet known to have liquid water on its surface.
