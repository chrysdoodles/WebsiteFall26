BASIC FUN FACTS Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun. It is the second-largest planet in our Solar System. Saturn is about 4.5 billion years old. Saturn is a gas giant. A year on Saturn lasts about 29 Earth years. A day on Saturn lasts about 10.7 hours. Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings. Saturn's rings are made mostly of ice and rock. Saturn has many moons, including Titan. Saturn is less dense than water.
