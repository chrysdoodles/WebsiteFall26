BASIC FUN FACTS The Sun is a star at the center of our Solar System. It is about 4.6 billion years old. The Sun is about 93 million miles (150 million km) away from Earth. It contains about 99.8% of the total mass of the Solar System. The Sun is mostly made of hydrogen and helium. Its diameter is about 864,000 miles (1.39 million km). You could fit about 1.3 million Earths inside the Sun by volume. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.
