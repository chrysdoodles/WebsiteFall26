BASIC FUN FACTS Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun. It is the largest planet in our Solar System. Jupiter is about 4.6 billion years old. Jupiter is a gas giant. A year on Jupiter lasts about 12 Earth years. A day on Jupiter lasts only about 10 hours. Jupiter has a very strong magnetic field. Jupiter has many moons, including the four large Galilean moons. Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a giant storm. Jupiter is mostly made of hydrogen and helium.
