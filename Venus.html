BASIC FUN FACTS Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is about 4.5 billion years old. Venus is similar in size to Earth. A year on Venus lasts about 225 Earth days. A day on Venus lasts about 243 Earth days. Venus is the hottest planet in our Solar System. Temperatures on Venus can reach about 900 degrees Fahrenheit. Venus has a very thick atmosphere. Venus is covered by thick clouds made mostly of sulfuric acid. Venus rotates in the opposite direction of most planets.
