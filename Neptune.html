BASIC FUN FACTS Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. It is the fourth-largest planet in our Solar System. Neptune is about 4.5 billion years old. Neptune is an ice giant. A year on Neptune lasts about 165 Earth years. A day on Neptune lasts about 16 hours. Neptune has extremely strong winds. Neptune has a deep blue color. Neptune has rings. Neptune has many moons, including Triton.
