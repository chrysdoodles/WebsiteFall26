BASIC FUN FACTS Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It is the smallest planet in our Solar System. Mercury is about 4.5 billion years old. Mercury is about 36 million miles (58 million km) away from the Sun. A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days. A day on Mercury lasts about 59 Earth days. Mercury has a very thin atmosphere. Temperatures on Mercury can change dramatically. Mercury has a rocky surface covered with craters.
