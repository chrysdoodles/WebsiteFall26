Welcome to Mars

BASIC FUN FACTS Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It is about 4.5 billion years old. Mars is often called the Red Planet. Mars gets its red color from iron minerals in its soil. A year on Mars lasts about 687 Earth days. A day on Mars lasts about 24 hours and 37 minutes. Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos. Mars has the largest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons. Mars also has a huge canyon called Valles Marineris. Mars has a very thin atmosphere.
