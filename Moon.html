BASIC FUN FACTS The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It is about 4.5 billion years old. The Moon is about 238,900 miles (384,400 km) away from Earth. The Moon is much smaller than Earth. The Moon's gravity is about 1/6 of Earth's gravity. The Moon has a diameter of about 2,159 miles (3,474 km). The Moon does not produce its own light. The Moon reflects light from the Sun. It takes about 27.3 days for the Moon to orbit Earth.
