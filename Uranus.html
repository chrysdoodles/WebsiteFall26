BASIC FUN FACTS Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. It is the third-largest planet in our Solar System. Uranus is about 4.5 billion years old. Uranus is an ice giant. A year on Uranus lasts about 84 Earth years. A day on Uranus lasts about 17 hours. Uranus rotates on its side. Uranus has a blue-green color because of methane in its atmosphere. Uranus has rings. Uranus has many moons, including Titania and Oberon.
